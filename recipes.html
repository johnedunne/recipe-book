Lemon Garlic Roast Chicken Chicken French 1 hr 30 min Easy 4 https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/6d/Good_Food_Display_-_NCI_Visuals_Online.jpg/320px-Good_Food_Display_-_NCI_Visuals_Online.jpg A golden, crispy-skinned roast chicken bursting with lemon and garlic. The perfect Sunday dinner that looks impressive but needs almost no effort. Pat the chicken completely dry before roasting — this is the secret to really crispy skin.
  • 1 whole, chicken
  • 4 cloves, garlic
  • 2, lemon
  • 3 tbsp, olive oil
  • 1 tsp, salt
  • 1 tsp, black pepper
  • Small bunch, fresh rosemary
  • 1, onion
    •
  • Preheat your oven to 200°C (400°F / Gas Mark 6).
  • Pat the chicken completely dry with paper towels, inside and out.
  • Cut the lemons in half and crush the garlic cloves with the flat of a knife.
  • Stuff the cavity with lemon halves, garlic, and rosemary sprigs.
  • Rub the outside of the chicken all over with olive oil, then season generously with salt and pepper.
  • Place on a rack in a roasting tin. Surround with halved onions.
  • Roast for 20 minutes per 500g plus an extra 20 minutes (roughly 1 hr 20 min for a 1.8kg bird).
  • Check doneness by piercing the thigh — juices should run completely clear.
  • Rest uncovered for 10–15 minutes before carving and serving.

    Spaghetti Carbonara Pasta Italian 25 min Medium 2 The real Roman carbonara — no cream, just eggs, pecorino, guanciale and pasta water creating a silky, rich sauce that clings to every strand. The heat of the pasta cooks the eggs. Remove the pan from the heat before adding the egg mixture to avoid scrambling.
  • 200g, spaghetti
  • 100g, guanciale
  • 2 large, egg
  • 1, egg yolk
  • 50g, pecorino romano
  • 50g, parmesan
  • 1 tsp, black pepper
  • Salt, for pasta water
    •
  • Bring a large pot of heavily salted water to the boil.
  • While it heats, cut the guanciale into small cubes or strips.
  • In a bowl, whisk together the eggs, egg yolk, finely grated pecorino, and parmesan. Season with plenty of black pepper.
  • Fry the guanciale in a wide pan over medium heat until golden and the fat has rendered. Turn off the heat.
  • Cook the spaghetti until al dente. Reserve at least a mug of pasta cooking water before draining.
  • Add the drained pasta to the guanciale pan and toss well off the heat.
  • Add the egg and cheese mixture and toss vigorously, adding a splash of pasta water to loosen into a creamy sauce.
  • Serve immediately with extra grated pecorino and a crack of black pepper.

    Simple Tomato & Basil Soup Soup Italian 35 min Easy 4 A velvety, vibrant tomato soup made from pantry staples. Roasting the tomatoes first concentrates their sweetness beautifully. Add a small pinch of sugar if your tomatoes taste too acidic.
  • 800g, tomato
  • 1, onion
  • 4 cloves, garlic
  • 2 tbsp, olive oil
  • 500ml, vegetable stock
  • Small bunch, fresh basil
  • 1 tsp, salt
  • 1/2 tsp, black pepper
  • 1 tsp, sugar
    •
  • Preheat oven to 180°C (350°F). Halve the tomatoes and place cut-side up on a baking tray.
  • Scatter over the whole garlic cloves (unpeeled), drizzle with olive oil, and season with salt and pepper.
  • Roast for 25 minutes until soft and slightly caramelised at the edges.
  • Meanwhile, soften the diced onion in a large saucepan with a little olive oil over medium-low heat, about 10 minutes.
  • Squeeze the roasted garlic out of its skins into the onion pan. Add the roasted tomatoes and all their juices.
  • Pour in the vegetable stock and bring to a gentle simmer for 5 minutes.
  • Add the fresh basil leaves, then blend until completely smooth with a stick blender.
  • Taste and adjust seasoning. Serve with crusty bread and a drizzle of olive oil.
